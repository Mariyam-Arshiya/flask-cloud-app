The problem

Every platform only shows the wins.

LinkedIn shows the promotion, not the 40 rejections. Twitter shows the launch, not the 6 abandoned versions. We only see finished, polished, working things. And it makes us feel like we are the only ones struggling. InkReal is built on a simple idea: your dead ends are as valuable as your wins. Sometimes more.

How it works

Three things nobody else does.

The core mechanics that make InkReal different from every other writing platform.

01 / DEAD ENDS

Post what did not work

A dedicated post type for abandoned drafts and failed experiments. Structured with what you tried, what failed, and what you learned. Not buried as a footnote.

02 / REVISIONS

Every edit is visible

Every post shows its full revision history. v1 through v-whatever. Others see how your thinking evolved, not just the polished final version.

03 / VERIFIED

Link your GitHub

Optional OAuth link to your GitHub. When you post about code, others see your real work as supporting proof. No AI-detection theatre. Real signals only.
